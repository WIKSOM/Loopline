How to use Loopline

A quick rundown of everything Loopline can do:

Getting started
#

Channels

Pick a channel on the left to switch topics. Tap "＋ New channel" to start your own — unread channels show a small badge.

💬

Sending messages

Type in the box at the bottom and hit send or press Enter. Everyone in that channel sees it appear right away.

Make it your own
😊

Emoji & GIFs

Use the 😊 and GIF buttons next to the message box. GIF search is live and organized into quick categories like Funny, Sad, or Congrats.

✍️

Typing indicator

When someone's writing a reply, "is typing…" shows up just above the message box — and they'll see the same for you.

Good to know
✕

Deleting a message

Tap one of your own messages to reveal a small ✕, then tap it to delete. It's hidden the rest of the time so it doesn't get in the way.

🔔

Notifications

Sound and in-app alerts are on by default. Tap the bell to turn them off, and your browser may separately ask permission for background alerts.

🛠️

Developer tools

The locked Developer panel in the sidebar is for site admins only — it's what allows clearing a channel's messages or deleting a channel entirely.
